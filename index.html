Why Jordi exists

The Building Safety Act created a role with no dedicated tool.

Every notifiable project in England now requires a Building Regulations Principal Designer — a named professional with personal liability who must demonstrate compliance at three regulatory Gateways. Until now, BRPDs have managed this with spreadsheets, annotated PDFs, and email chains. Jordi changes that.

⚡

Start before the drawing board

Ask Jordi tells you exactly which Parts of the Building Regulations apply to your project — free, in under two minutes, before design work begins.

🔍

Catch issues at Stage 2, not Stage 4

Jordi Lite flags Part B and Part M compliance issues at early design stage, when a corridor width is a five-minute fix rather than a three-week redesign.

📋

Gateway-ready from day one

Module 3 assembles formal compliance drawing sets — floor plan with regulation extract adjacent — in the format the Building Safety Regulator requires.

🔗

The Golden Thread, built in

Every compliance report and drawing set is timestamped, version-controlled, and mapped to the relevant RIBA Work Stage. The audit trail builds itself.

The Building Safety Act 2022 changed everything for building designers.

The Act came into full force on 1 October 2023 — the most significant overhaul of UK building regulations since the 1980s. For the first time, a named professional on every notifiable project carries personal legal liability for demonstrating compliance at each regulatory Gateway. No dedicated tool existed for this role. Until Jordi.
