Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Concept Engine Ltd collects, uses, and protects personal data submitted through the Jordi website at heyjordi.com.

Concept Engine Ltd· Last updated June 2026

Who we are

Jordi is a product of Concept Engine Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales.

Registered address: LCB Depot, 31 Rutland Street, Leicester, LE1 1RE

Contact: team@heyjordi.com

For the purposes of the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, Concept Engine Ltd is the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

What data we collect

We collect personal data through two forms on this website:

Launch notification form

  • Email address

Beta programme application form

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Job role or profession
  • Company name

We do not collect any other personal data through this website. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics tools.

Why we collect it

We collect this data for the following purposes:

  • Launch notification — to send you a single email when Jordi launches publicly. You will receive no other emails unless you separately opt in.
  • Beta programme — to assess your application for early access to the Jordi platform and to communicate with you about your application and participation.

The legal basis for processing this data is consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You provide this data voluntarily by submitting one of the forms on this website.

How we store and protect your data

Your data is stored securely with MailerLite, our email marketing platform. MailerLite is GDPR-compliant and processes data in accordance with its own privacy policy, available at mailerlite.com.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

How long we keep your data

We will retain your data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Launch notification subscribers — until the launch email has been sent, after which data will be deleted or you will be offered the opportunity to remain on a future communications list.
  • Beta programme applicants — for the duration of the beta programme and up to 12 months after its conclusion.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at team@heyjordi.com.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • The right to access the data we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time
  • The right to object to processing
  • The right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at team@heyjordi.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully.

Unsubscribing

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You may unsubscribe at any time. Unsubscribing will remove you from all future communications from Jordi.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect when it was last updated. Continued use of this website following any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at team@heyjordi.com and we will respond within 30 days.
